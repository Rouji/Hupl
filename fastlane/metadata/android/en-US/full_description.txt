Hupl is a simple file uploader for Android. Share files from any app directly to your configured HTTP upload servers.

Features:
* Upload files via the Android share menu
* Configure multiple upload servers with custom parameters
* Import/export server configurations as JSON
* Keep a history of uploads and generated links
* Optional image resize/compression before upload
* Works with most simple filehosts (POST-based uploads)
* Preconfigured uploaders for popular services
* No tracking, no ads, no accounts

All your upload history and host configurations are stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to third parties except the files you explicitly choose to upload.
